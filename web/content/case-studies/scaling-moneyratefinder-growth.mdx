## Reframing the Problem: Traffic → Intent Coverage

Old Question

How do we increase traffic?

Chasing volume. No targeting. 78% bounce rate.

New Question

How do we capture more high-intent searches at scale?

Engineering intent. Precision at volume.

> **What this showed.** We stopped asking "how do we get more traffic" and started asking "how do we own every combination of high-intent search that exists in our market."

## What Actually Worked

### We Built a System, Not Content

01

Competitors wrote articles. We built an intent capture machine. Articles age and require maintenance. Systems compound. Our 5,000-page architecture kept ranking for new long-tail variations we didn't even target explicitly.

### Utility > Words

02

Users searching for remittance rates want ONE thing: the best rate right now. Not a 1,500-word blog post about the history of remittance. Pages that acted like tools — with live comparison tables and instant answers — converted at 6× the rate of informational pages.

### Speed Was SEO

03

Every 100ms of page load improvement correlated with measurable ranking gains. Fast pages ranked better, bounced less, and converted better. Our CDN + caching strategy meant corridor pages loaded in under 800ms globally.

### Internal Linking Was the Hidden Weapon

04

The internal linking graph — connecting every corridor to related corridors, providers, and currency pages — did three things: boosted page indexing speed, improved rankings through PageRank distribution, and reduced crawl depth so Google could efficiently discover all 5,000+ pages.

## Challenges (Real Ones)

### Indexing Delays

New pages weren't getting indexed quickly enough — thousands of pages sat uncrawled for weeks.

Sitemap batching with priority signals + dense internal links to new pages + Google Search Console re-submission pipeline.

border-orange-200 bg-orange-50

### Thin Content Risk

Pages for similar corridors looked too similar — risking a thin content penalty from Google.

Dynamic insight layers (different trend callouts, fee comparisons, and provider recommendations per corridor) made each page genuinely different.

border-red-200 bg-red-50

### Infrastructure Load

5,000 pages with live data = heavy server load, especially during peak transfer hours.

CDN + aggressive caching (rates cached 5 min, fees 24hr) + a static-dynamic hybrid: page shell cached, live data injected client-side.
